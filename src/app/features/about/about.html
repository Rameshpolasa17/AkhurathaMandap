Our story

Built by the people who celebrate here

There is no organising company behind {{ mandapName }} and no paid staff. Every year the pandal goes up because neighbours take leave from work to build it, the mahaprasad is served because families cook through the night, and the cultural evenings happen because local artists give their time.

What began as a small street celebration has grown into eleven days that the whole neighbourhood plans its year around — and the way it is run has not changed at all.
