Good to know

Before you take part

A few things that apply to every competition at the mandap. If anything is unclear, send us a message and the committee will help.

Ask the committee
  • Entry is free for every competition — there is no participation fee.
  • Please arrive fifteen minutes before the listed start time.
  • Participants under twelve should be accompanied by a parent or guardian.
  • Content and costumes must be suitable for a family festival.
  • The judges' decision is final for every category.
  • Photographs taken during the competitions may appear in the mandap gallery.
